title: "PrivateProviderDataPolicy"

Defaults to training on and retaining prompts, the most conservative declaration. Your own declaration; it applies to your own traffic and OpenRouter does not verify it.


## Fields

| Field                                                                       | Type                                                                        | Required                                                                    | Description                                                                 |
| --------------------------------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| `PromptRetentionDays`                                                       | `*int64`                                                                    | ✔️                                                          | Days prompts are retained. Must be `null` unless `retains_prompts` is true. |
| `RetainsPrompts`                                                            | `bool`                                                                      | ✔️                                                          | Whether the provider retains prompts or completions.                        |
| `Training`                                                                  | `bool`                                                                      | ✔️                                                          | Whether the provider trains on prompts.                                     |
